About HERA

HERA is a patient-owned AI health memory for women. It turns everyday health experiences into a structured, longitudinal story you can understand, track, compare, prepare with, control, and share. HERA organizes what you tell it — it never diagnoses, prescribes, or recommends treatment. You always decide what's saved, edited, or shared.
